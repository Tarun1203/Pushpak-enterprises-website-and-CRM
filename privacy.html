Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026 · Applies to this website and the My Account portal

We collect only what we need to register your product, book and carry out service, and answer your enquiries. We do not sell your data, we do not run advertising or tracking, and we do not profile you.

1. Who we are

Pushpak Enterprises, Hubli, Karnataka ("we", "us") distributes and services MakWell, Flyvision and Skevia products. For the personal data described here we decide why and how it is used (the "data fiduciary" under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023).

3. What we do not collect

  • No advertising, analytics, session-recording or social-media tracking code runs on this website.
  • No contacts, camera or microphone access. Your location is read only if you tap "Use my location" in the service-center finder, is used only in your browser, and is never stored or sent to us.
  • No government ID numbers, bank or card details, or payment data on this website.
  • No data about children, and no sensitive categories such as health or religion.

4. Who we share it with

  • Our authorised service centres, technicians, dealers and warehouse staff — only the details needed for the job they are assigned.
  • Our service providers (section 5), who process data for us under their own security terms.
  • Authorities, where the law requires it.

We do not sell or rent your data and do not share it for marketing by others.

6. How long we keep it

  • Registration and service records: for the warranty period plus three years, so we can handle claims and disputes.
  • Enquiries that do not become a customer record: up to 12 months.
  • Records we must keep for tax or accounting law: for the period that law requires.

After that we delete the record or remove the details that identify you.

7. Your rights

You can ask us to: tell you what we hold about you; correct or complete it; erase it (unless the law or an open warranty claim requires us to keep it); withdraw your consent; or nominate someone to exercise these rights for you if you cannot. Email or call us using the details below, from the mobile number you registered. We will reply within 30 days. If you are not satisfied you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India once it is operational.

8. Security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest by our hosting provider. Access inside our team is limited by role: a technician sees only the jobs assigned to them, and a service centre sees only its own. Staff sign in with individual accounts. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affecting your data occurs we will notify you and the authorities as the law requires.

9. Children

This website is for adults. Please do not submit information about anyone under 18. If you believe a child's details were sent to us, contact us and we will delete them.

10. Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will update the date above and, if the change affects what you agreed to, ask for your consent again.

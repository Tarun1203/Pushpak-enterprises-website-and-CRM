Cookie Policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026 · Applies to this website and the My Account portal

This website uses no advertising or analytics cookies. It stores only the small items it needs to work, and your own cookie choice. You can change that choice at any time.

1. What are cookies and similar storage?

Cookies are small text files a website saves in your browser. Browsers also offer other local storage (such as localStorage and IndexedDB) that works the same way for this purpose. We call all of these "cookies" in this policy.

Not used: advertising cookies, analytics or measurement cookies, social-media pixels, or session recording. If we ever add any, they will stay off until you choose to allow them.

3. Your choices

  • Essential items are needed for the site to work and cannot be switched off here. Without them you could not sign in.
  • Optional categories (analytics, preferences) are currently not in use. The settings panel shows them switched off, and anything we add later will start switched off.
  • Use Cookie settings in the footer of any page to change your choice or withdraw it. Withdrawing is as easy as giving consent.
  • You can also delete cookies and site data in your browser settings; we will ask for your choice again next time.

4. Third parties

Only Google (Firebase Authentication and reCAPTCHA) can set or read items on the My Account page, and only so you can sign in securely. See the Privacy Policy for details. We host fonts ourselves, so no font provider sees your visit.

5. Changes

If we change what we store, we will update this table and the date above, and ask for your choice again when the change needs your consent.

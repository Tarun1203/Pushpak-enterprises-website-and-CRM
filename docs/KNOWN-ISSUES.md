# Known issues and limits (documented for go-live)

## Must be fixed or accepted in writing before go-live
- None found by the automated checks. Pending: the confirmations in GO-LIVE.md.

## Documented, low risk
- **Offline:** the CRM needs internet. Offline shows a red bar; nothing is queued. Do not retype a save repeatedly; check the record after the connection returns.
- **Browsers:** tested automatically on Chrome, Firefox, Safari (WebKit), Android Chrome, iPhone Safari, iPad. Edge uses the Chrome engine and is not tested separately. Internet Explorer is not supported.
- **PDF:** "PDF" is the browser's Print → Save as PDF. Thermal slips use a fixed 80 × 200 mm page; the printer driver cuts to content on most roll printers.
- **Customer confirmation before closing a job** is not built; the voucher has a signature line only.
- **Sales and Accounts roles** do not exist; Admin (Head Office) does those jobs. Area Manager page is not live.
- **No screen to switch an account off** (`disabledByAdmin`) other than setting the technician/center status; Head Office-level switch needs a console edit.
- **SLA timers** run in the browser/scheduled sweep only, not as a hard server block.
- **Spare dispatch stock accounting** for warehouse is done by the client, not enforced server-side.
- **Job/claim IDs** are generated by the client for some records (duplicates are detected and flagged, not prevented).
- **WhatsApp:** the button opens WhatsApp with the message prepared. Nothing is sent automatically.
- **Backups and monitoring** are documented but must be switched on by the owner (BACKUP-RECOVERY.md, MONITORING.md).

## Future enhancements
Offline queue for technicians, automatic WhatsApp sending, server-generated IDs everywhere, client-error logging into Firestore, customer e-signature.

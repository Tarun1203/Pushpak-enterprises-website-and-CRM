About Pushpak Enterprises

Pushpak Enterprises is a Karnataka-based home appliances distribution business, bringing together three brands — MakWell, Flyvision and Skevia — under one company and one service standard.

From geysers and washing machines to newer product lines, every item we sell is backed by product registration, tracked warranty, and a service network built to reach customers directly — not just at the point of sale.
